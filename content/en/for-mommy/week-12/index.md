title: Week 12 of Pregnancy

Reaching week 12 is a significant milestone in your pregnancy journey. At this point, your baby’s main organs have been formed, and now the focus is on growth. Weighing about 20 grams, your baby is continuing to develop rapidly. From this week onward, it’s often possible to listen to your baby’s heartbeat, marking a memorable moment in your pregnancy.

<!--more-->

## **What's happening with your baby at 12 weeks**

Your baby’s heart is beating at a rate of 110 to 160 beats per minute, and you may notice the chest beginning to move up and down as the baby practices future breathing movements. Although these are just practice breaths, they are essential for lung development. Your baby's facial features are becoming more defined, but they’re still too small to be seen clearly on a scan. Meanwhile, the pituitary gland, located beneath the brain, has begun producing hormones, allowing your baby to start regulating its own growth and development.

With the primary organs now fully formed and in place, the next few weeks will be dedicated to growth. This period, often considered as the most critical phase of your baby’s development, is nearly complete. Your baby’s fingers and toes are now fully formed, and tiny nails are beginning to grow. Your baby may even start sucking on a thumb, a reflex they’ll continue to practice. Additionally, your baby is swallowing and urinating amniotic fluid, an important process for learning how to swallow and drink. Remarkably, the amniotic fluid is entirely replaced every day, ensuring that waste is continually removed.

## **What’s happening to your body at week 12**

As your pregnancy progresses, you might notice some skin changes, which are common during this time. Pregnancy acne, stretch marks, a darkening of the skin known as the pregnancy mask, and itchy skin are all normal occurrences. These changes are due to the hormonal shifts your body is experiencing and are common among pregnant women.

## **What to do in week 12**

*1. Announce your pregnancy*

Now that you’ve reached the 12-week mark, you’ve crossed an important threshold. With almost one-third of your pregnancy behind you, the risk of miscarriage has significantly decreased. Since most miscarriages occur between the eighth and eleventh weeks, this is an excellent time to share your exciting news with friends and family if you haven’t done so already.

*2. Focus on healthy and varied eating*

Maintaining a healthy and varied diet is crucial, especially now. Some women may notice unusual cravings or aversions, which can signal a need for specific nutrients. For instance, a craving for something salty might indicate a need for fish or unrefined sea salt. It’s important to listen to your body, as it often knows what it needs. Be mindful of your sugar intake, as excessive sugar consumption during pregnancy can pose risks. Additionally, make sure you stay hydrated, but be careful not to overdo it with water, as moderation is key.

Week 12 is a time of growth and transition for both you and your baby. As you move forward, remember to take care of yourself, eat well, and enjoy this remarkable journey.
